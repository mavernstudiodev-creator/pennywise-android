Thoughtful tools for everyday money

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

From the first allowance of the month to your next savings goal, Pennywise keeps your financial picture organised and easier to understand.

01 / TRACK

Income & expense tracking

Record money coming in and going out. Organise transactions with categories, dates and notes.

02 / ORGANISE

Allowance management

Keep track of recurring and one-time allowances so you know what money is available to work with.

03 / PLAN

Budgets that make sense

Set a spending limit, monitor your progress and see how your expenses compare with your plan.

04 / UNDERSTAND

Spending insights

Review category patterns, daily averages and planned versus unplanned spending over time.

05 / REFLECT

Behavioural reflections

Optionally note what influenced a purchase and how you felt about it. No judgement, just awareness.

06 / GROW

Savings goals & reports

Set a savings target, follow your progress and review your activity with useful summaries.

Privacy & control

Your financial life stays personal.

Pennywise is designed around an offline-first experience for personal money tracking. Core tracking is intended to work on your device without requiring an account.

✓No account required for core tracking
✓Designed for local, on-device records
✓Behavioural reflections are optional
✓You choose what information to enter

Please review the app's current permissions and privacy information before entering sensitive financial information. This page describes the intended product approach and is not a security certification.

Ask a question ↗
What is Pennywise?

Pennywise is a student-focused personal finance app designed to help college students track income and expenses, manage allowances, understand spending patterns and work towards savings goals.

Is Pennywise free to download?

The Android v1.0.0 APK is available through the project's GitHub Releases page. Check the release page for the current version and distribution details.

Which devices are supported?

This release is distributed as an Android APK. Compatibility can depend on your Android version and device. iOS is not currently offered through this download.

Does the app need an internet connection?

Pennywise is designed with offline-first core tracking in mind. Internet access may be needed to visit the download page or access online release information.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is intended to be required for the core personal tracking experience.

Are behavioural reflections compulsory?

No. Reflections are optional. You can record a transaction without answering questions about why you made the purchase or how you felt.

How do I install the Android app?

Download the APK from the official GitHub release, open it from your Downloads folder and follow Android's installation prompts. Only install it if you trust the source and understand any security warnings. Never bypass an unresolved warning.

How do I get the latest version?

Use the GitHub Releases page linked below to check the latest published version and its release notes.

Can Pennywise connect to my bank?

This website does not claim bank connectivity. The listed product focus is manual income and expense tracking.
