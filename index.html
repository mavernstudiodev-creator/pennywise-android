Everything in one place

More awareness.
Less money stress.

Pennywise brings everyday money tracking and behavioural awareness together in a focused experience designed around student allowances, expenses and savings.

01 / TRACK

Income & expenses

Record money coming in and going out. Add a category, date and note so your activity stays clear and organised.

02 / PLAN

Allowance & budgets

Manage recurring or one-time allowances, set budgets and see how much of your available budget remains.

03 / UNDERSTAND

Spending insights

Explore daily averages, category patterns, planned versus unplanned purchases and budget utilisation.

04 / REFLECT

Behavioural reflections

Optionally note what influenced a purchase and how it felt. Reflection is there to build awareness, never to judge.

05 / SAVE

Savings goals

Create a savings target, track progress and keep contributions separate from everyday expenses.

06 / REVIEW

Reports & history

Review transactions and spending over time to make more informed decisions about your money.

A simple routine

Small steps. Stronger habits.

You do not need to be a finance expert. Start with what you have and let the picture become clearer over time.

STEP 01

Set your starting point

Add your allowance or income and decide on a budget that makes sense for your month.

STEP 02

Log as you go

Record purchases and income. Add an optional reflection when you want to understand the reason behind a decision.

STEP 03

Review and adjust

Use your activity and insights to spot patterns, adjust your budget and work towards a savings goal.

Your money. Your records.

Designed around personal control.

Pennywise is designed as an offline-first personal finance companion. Core money tracking is intended to work on your device without requiring an account.

✓No account required for the core experience
✓Designed for local, on-device record keeping
✓Behavioural reflections are optional
✓You control the information you choose to enter

Privacy note: these describe the intended product design. Review the app's current permissions and privacy information before entering sensitive financial information.
